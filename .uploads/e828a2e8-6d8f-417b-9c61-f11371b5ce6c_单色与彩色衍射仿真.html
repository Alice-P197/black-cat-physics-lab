衍射原理公式

圆形孔径（艾里斑）
$$I(\theta) = I_0\left[\frac{2J_1(\beta)}{\beta}\right]^2,\quad \beta = \frac{\pi D\sin\theta}{\lambda}$$
单缝衍射
$$I(\theta) = I_0\left[\frac{\sin\alpha}{\alpha}\right]^2,\quad \alpha = \frac{\pi a\sin\theta}{\lambda}$$
双缝干涉 + 衍射
$$I = I_0\left[\frac{\sin\alpha}{\alpha}\right]^2\cos^2\gamma,\quad \gamma = \frac{\pi d\sin\theta}{\lambda}$$
方形孔径
$$I = I_0\,\mathrm{sinc}^2\!\left(\frac{\pi a\sin\theta_x}{\lambda}\right) \mathrm{sinc}^2\!\left(\frac{\pi a\sin\theta_y}{\lambda}\right)$$
